# Let Competitors create Competition Offers

Eligible Competitors, rather than the Protocol Operator, will create open or directed Competition Offers from approved immutable Round templates. The first compatible acceptance creates a Draft Round. After both Competitors complete readiness, the protocol freezes the Round start and creates its empty per-market FPMM; the first valid eligible community LP deposit makes AMM execution available under ADR 0017, but the Round itself does not depend on reaching a liquidity target. This gives the product game-like matchmaking and removes manual pairing or liquidity power from the operator, while a one-active-commitment limit, atomic acceptance, short expiries, account-relationship checks, and immutable post-acceptance terms contain spam, concurrency, collusion, and rule-selection risks.

ADR 0019 preserves self-service open and directed offers but replaces Competitors and Round templates with Participants, Competition Templates, Draft Competitions, and fixed-duration Live Sessions.
