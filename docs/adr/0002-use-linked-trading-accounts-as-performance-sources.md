# Support Centralized Sources and On-chain Sources

Competitors will keep trading through their existing accounts, and the protocol will support both Centralized Sources through read-only account access and On-chain Sources through wallet proof plus public blockchain data. Both source classes normalize into the same Performance Record without moving competitor capital into protocol custody; this preserves competitor choice and avoids operating a trading venue, at the cost of two integration models, difficult cross-source normalization, external-data trust, privacy controls, and a challengeable oracle path for on-chain Resolution.

ADR 0019 replaces the universal trading-account model with template-specific Linked Competition Accounts and Competition Sources. Trading accounts may return under a later trading Competition Template.
