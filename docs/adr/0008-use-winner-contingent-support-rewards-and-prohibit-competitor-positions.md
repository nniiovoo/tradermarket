# Use winner-contingent Support Rewards and prohibit Competitor positions

The MVP's 1% AMM purchase fee will allocate 75% to Liquidity Providers and escrow 25% for the selected Competitor, who may claim only the final winning payout fraction while the remainder returns to the original fee payers; sales pay their full fee to Liquidity Providers and the Protocol Operator takes no trading fee. Because a Competitor can influence the underlying result, Competitors and disclosed related wallets may hold no Outcome Position in their own Round and must post an Integrity Bond forfeitable only for objectively proven, pre-published violations, aligning rewards with winning without pretending an oracle can infer intent or discover every concealed wallet.

ADR 0013 replaces the selected-side 75/25 fee split with a whole-market Winner Reward Pool funded by purchases on both sides. The prohibition on Competitor and related-wallet positions remains in force.
